## Adversarial Review: 3 Genuine Weaknesses

### Weakness 1 — Spectral calibration artefact
- ALMA data retracted Oct 2020 by NRAO due to calibration pipeline error
- Inflated signal by ~2×; reprocessed data far weaker
- 267 GHz feature overlaps with SO₂ doublet within instrument uncertainty

### Weakness 2 — Abundance overstated
- Headline "~20 ppb" derived from uncorrected ALMA data
- Post-correction reanalyses: Snellen et al. (2020), Villanueva et al. (2020) → upper bound <1–7 ppb
- At <7 ppb: abiotic sources (volcanism, lightning, photochemistry) cannot be ruled out

### Weakness 3 — Abiotic pathways insufficiently excluded
- Reducing volcanism (phosphide-bearing magmas) not observationally excluded
- Bains et al. (2020) rebuttal showed UV photochemical reduction of phosphates viable at ppb levels
- "Life is the only explanation" is logically premature given poor constraints on Venus deep geochemistry

## Minor Points (also included)
- Temporal variability: 2 snapshots months apart — cannot distinguish seasonal/dynamic abiotic source
- Altitude profile not constrained — changes photochemical destruction rate requirement
- Statistical significance quoted against thermal noise only (systematic errors excluded)
